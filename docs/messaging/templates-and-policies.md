# Template Examples and Messaging Policy

Templates must be reviewed against the chosen provider's current rules before use. Use only approved templates where required.

## English example
“Hello {{guest_name}}, you’re invited to {{function_name}} on {{event_date}} at {{venue}}. Please confirm how many people from your invited group will attend: {{rsvp_link}}. If you have already responded, no action is needed.”

## Hindi example
“नमस्ते {{guest_name}}, आपको {{event_date}} को {{venue}} में आयोजित {{function_name}} में आमंत्रित किया गया है। कृपया अपने आमंत्रित समूह में आने वाले लोगों की संख्या यहाँ बताएं: {{rsvp_link}}। यदि आप पहले ही जवाब दे चुके हैं, तो दोबारा जवाब देने की आवश्यकता नहीं है।”

## Policy
- No marketing add-on by default.
- Verify channel-specific authorization, template and opt-out rules before sending.
- Show event name and sender identity clearly.
- Use localized content and safe URL domains.
- Do not include sensitive details in message body.
- Record template version and provider message ID.

## Research update (2026-10-27)
- Do not assume a guest spreadsheet grants permission to send WhatsApp/SMS messages. Capture organizer attestation and the applicable authorization/consent evidence; suppress opted-out contacts.
- Business-initiated WhatsApp messages may require approved templates under current policy; service-window rules, categories and pricing must be verified from current official sources/provider documentation.
- Estimate message spend before dispatch and require explicit confirmation when campaigns have material cost. Keep per-event and tenant caps.
- References: https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ ; https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/ ; https://www.trai.gov.in/advice-to-senders
